# Frontend Design

Approach design as the lead at a design studio known for giving every project a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. Avoid cliché or templated design patterns: make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to the brief, and take aesthetic risks when justified.

---

## 1. Ground Designs in the Subject Matter

- **Identify the Subject & Audience First**: Before writing UI code, define who this is for and what the primary job of the interface is.
- **Draw from the Domain's Vernacular**: A developer infrastructure tool, a fintech terminal, an editorial publication, a luxury boutique, and an educational platform should feel completely distinct in density, texture, tone, and pacing.
- **Real, Specific Content**: Never use generic placeholder lorem ipsum or generic buzzword soup. Craft realistic, compelling copy tailored directly to the subject matter.

---

## 2. Avoid AI-Generated Design Tells & Cliches

Actively avoid these common AI design cliches:
- **Random Single-Word Accents**: Don't accent just a single word in a headline with a neon gradient or italics.
- **Unnecessary All-Caps Labels & Badges**: Don't clutter sections with uppercase pill tags or eyebrow chips unless strictly meaningful.
- **Gratuitous Numbered Steps (01 / 02 / 03)**: Only use numbered sequences if the content is truly a chronological step-by-step process.
- **Overused Generic Glowing Cards**: Avoid the standard dark-mode card with identical purple/cyan radial blur behind everything unless deliberately fitting.
- **Scattered Motion**: Don't attach bouncy entrance animations or hover transforms to every card. Keep animations purposeful and tied to user actions.

---

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy

- **Personality Through Type**: Typography carries the character of the page. Select intentional font pairings (e.g., crisp geometric sans, refined editorial serifs, technical monospace) with deliberate weights, tracking, and leading.
- **Disciplined Type Scale**: Establish clear visual contrast between headlines, subheads, and body copy.
- **Comfortable Line Lengths**: Keep paragraphs under 75–80 characters per line for high readability. Give serif type slightly more line height.

---

## 4. Color, Texture & Visual Structure

- **Curated Color Palettes**: Pick a focused, intentional palette (e.g., deep midnight with warm amber accents, crisp editorial monochrome with stark vermilion, or restrained slate and cobalt).
- **Structural Devices as Information**: Borders, dividers, subtle surfaces, and containers should organize information clearly rather than serve as mere decoration.
- **Negative Space & Rhythm**: Use generous, intentional whitespace to let high-impact elements breathe.

---

## 5. Design Workflow

1. **Understand & Define**: Establish the specific product identity, target user, and key action.
2. **Curate the System**: Define typefaces, color tokens, and layout density.
3. **Build Core Components**: Structure semantic, clean, accessible HTML/React components.
4. **Refine & Critique**: Check against generic design cliches, verify responsive behavior, and ensure typography and contrast are sharp.
